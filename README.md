# Nervousness before an exam

After working for twenty years, Mr Tan decided to return to studying. He was taking an important examination for the first time in twenty years, and he have been preparing for it for several months. Every night after work, he would sit at his desk and revise his notes. Sometimes, he felt confident, but on other days, he feel that he had forgotten everything he learned. 

As the examination date came closer, Mr Tan became increasingly nervous. He often woke up in the middle of the night, wondering if he would be able to remember what he had studied. On the morning of the examination, he arrived early and sat quietly outside the examination room. He looked around and saw many younger candidates. For a moment, he wonder if he had made the right decision. 

When the examiner finally opened the door, Mr Tan took a deep breath and walked inside. He was telling himself that he had done his best and there was nothing more to worry about.
